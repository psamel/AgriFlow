FRAMEWORK FOR AI USE CASE INTEGRATION IN CURRICULUM DELIVERY 
Part A: AI Use Case Profile 
1. Use Case ID: KJS-AGR-01 
2. Use Case Title: Irrigation Advisory System for Sugarcane Crop using AI and Sensor-based 
Technology 
3. AI Application Vertical: Agriculture 
4. Collaborating Organization:  K J Somaiya Applied Agricultural Research (KIAAR), 
Sameerwadi, Karnataka, Godavari Biorefineries Ltd. (GBL) 
5. Beneficiaries: Farmers (approx. 18,000–25,000 sugarcane farmers associated with GBL and 
KIAAR, Cane department su pervisors and field assistants, Agricultural researchers and 
agronomists, Sugar factory management for planning cane availability and productivity.  
6. Problem Statement:  Sugarcane cultivation requires  large quantities of water, and irrigation 
scheduling is generally based on farmers ’ experience rather than scientific recommendations. 
Due to inadequate knowledge of crop water requirements, soil moisture conditions, weather 
forecasts, and irrigation timi ng, farmers often practice over -irrigation or delayed irrigation, 
resulting in: 
 Water wastage 
 Reduced crop productivity 
 Yield loss due to moisture stress 
 Increased energy consumption 
 Higher cultivation costs 
 Nutrient losses because of improper fertigation 
 Inefficient use of electricity supplied for irrigation pumps 
 Difficulty in monitoring thousands of spatially distributed farms 
There is a need for an AI -driven irrigation advisory system that integrates field sensors, weather 
forecasts, geospatial information, and machine learning models to provide plot -specific irrigation 
recommendations and decision support to farmers in a simple and understandable manner. 
7. Background and Context:  
The KIAAR and GBL ecosystem supports nearly 18,000 –25,000 sugarcane farmers spread 
across Northern Karnataka. Through previous geofencing initiatives, thousands of sugarcane 
plots have already been digitized and spatially mapped. Soil testing records, farm er details, and 
crop information are available through existing digital platforms.  However, irrigation decisions 
are still largely manual and depend on experience rather than data -driven approaches. 
Variations in soil types, crop stages, weather conditions , rainfall events, and water availability 
necessitate dynamic irrigation recommendations. 
Recent advances in IoT and sensor technologies, Remote sensing, Artificial Intelligence and 
Machine Learning, Weather forecasting systems, Geospatial analytics, Mobil e applications and 
dashboards, etc. provide an opportunity to build a smart irrigation advisory system capable of 
delivering personalized recommendations at the plot level. 
The proposed system aims to combine sensor observations, satellite -derived informat ion, 
weather forecasts, and AI models to improve irrigation efficiency, enhance water productivity, 
and promote sustainable sugarcane cultivation.

8. Objectives:  
1) Develop an AI -enabled irrigation advisory system for sugarcane cultivation that integrates 
field observations, weather information, and crop -specific requirements to support informed 
irrigation decision-making. 
2) Monitor soil moisture, environmental conditions, and crop status using sensor networks and 
geospatial technologies to enable plot -level ass essment and precision irrigation 
management. 
3) Generate real -time, forecast -based, and plot -specific irrigation and fertigation 
recommendations to optimize irrigation scheduling, improve resource utilization, and support 
operational constraints such as electricity availability. 
4) Develop farmer-friendly web and mobile platforms for advisory dissemination, visualization of 
geofenced plots, crop information management, and decision support. 
5) Enhance water use efficiency, reduce yield losses due to water stress, and improve the 
sustainability and climate resilience of sugarcane farming practices. 
9. Solution Approach:  
The proposed system will integrate multiple data sources and AI models to provide intelligent  
irrigation recommendations: IoT Sensors, Geospatial Data, Weather Data, and Farm Information 
and develop the below listed AI Models for Prediction:  
 Next Irrigation Date Prediction 
 Irrigation Duration Prediction 
 Crop Water Requirement Prediction 
 Water Stress Probability Model 
 Rainfall-Adjusted Irrigation Recommendation 
 Yield Loss Prediction Due to Delayed Irrigation 
 Pump Scheduling Optimization 
 Fertigation Recommendation Model 
 Disease and Water Stress Forecasting 
 Yield Prediction Model 
 Farmer-Friendly Advisory Generation Model (LLM-Based) 
10. Operational Challenges:  
A. Sensor-related 
 Sensor calibration and maintenance.  
 Power supply and battery life.  
 Data communication failures.  
 Missing or noisy sensor data.  
B. AI Challenges 
 Limited labeled datasets.  
 Model generalization across soil types.  
 Seasonal variability.  
C. Geospatial Challenges 
 Accurate plot mapping.  
 Dynamic crop boundaries.  
 Multi-source data integration.  
D. Weather Challenges 
 Reliability of rainfall forecasts.  
 Micro-climatic variations.

E. Implementation Challenges 
 Connectivity issues in villages.  
 Farmer adoption and trust.  
 Device maintenance.  
 Training requirements.  
 Scalability to 25,000 farms.  
 Continuous model updating.  
 Integration with existing STEPS (Somaiya Technology for Efficient Plot Survey) platforms. 
11. High-Level Workflow:  
1) Data Acquisition: Collection of field, crop, soil, weather, and geospatial information through 
mobile applications, databases, APIs, sensor networks, IoT devices, satellite imagery, and 
GIS-based plot digitization. 
2) Data Integration: Integration of sensor observations, weather data, satellite imagery, soil 
reports, farmer records, and historical yield information into a unified agricultural data 
platform. 
3) AI Analytics: Development of AI/ML models for soil moisture forecasting, irrigation 
scheduling, water stress prediction, fertigation planning, pump scheduling optimization, yield 
loss estimation, and yield prediction. 
4) Decision Support: Generation of plot -specific irrigation and fertigation recommendations 
based on field conditions, crop requirements, weather information, and predictive analytics. 
5) Dashboard and Advisory Platform: Delivery of recommendations through web and mobile 
applications featuring farmer dashboards, spatial visualization of plots, irrigation and weather 
information, soil and crop health monitoring, fe rtigation guidance, yield analytics, and alert 
generation.

13. Modern AI Governance and Paradigms: 
Area Relevance Recommended Consideration 
Privacy & Data 
Protection High  
Ensure secure handling of farmer information, plot boundaries, 
sensor data, and soil reports through role -based access 
control, encryption, secure APIs, and data anonymization 
wherever required. Compliance with Digital Personal Data 
Protection (DPDP) Act and institutional data policies should be 
ensured. 
AI Security & 
Trustworthiness High 
Protect AI models and IoT infrastructure against cyber threats, 
unauthorized access, data tampering, and adversarial attacks. 
Implement secure communication protocols, model validation 
mechanisms, audit logs, and periodic security assessments to 
maintain reliability and trust in advisory recommendations. 
Responsible AI High 
Ensure transparency, fairness, explainability, and 
accountability in AI-generated irrigation advisories. Models 
should provide interpretable recommendations and avoid bias 
arising from variations in soil types, crop varieties, or 
geographic regions. Continuous monitoring and validation 
should be carried out before large-scale deployment. 
Generative AI / 
LLMs High 
Utilize Large Language Models (LLMs) to convert technical 
predictions into farmer-friendly multilingual advisories in 
English, Kannada, Marathi, and Hindi. Examples include 
recommendations such as “Irrigate tomorrow morning for 2.5 
hours” or “Rainfall is expected; irrigation is not required today.”
